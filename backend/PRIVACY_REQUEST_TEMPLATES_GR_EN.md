# Customer GDPR contact text — editable templates (Greek + English)

These are short **drafts**, NOT a published legal/privacy policy. Each business/controller must verify its own legal identity, contact channel, sources, retention and obligations. The processor Sendro does not replace the business's contact point. **No Sendro URL, unpublished inbox, or fabricated email address is required for a customer to exercise privacy rights.**

## 1. Business website / Privacy Policy text

**EN**

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held by **[BUSINESS NAME]**, or to exercise other applicable privacy rights, please email **[BUSINESS PRIVACY EMAIL]** or reply to our existing customer-service conversation. Please describe your request. For security, we may ask for information reasonably necessary to confirm your identity. We will respond within applicable legal time limits. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

**EL**

Για αιτήματα πρόσβασης, διόρθωσης ή διαγραφής προσωπικών δεδομένων που επεξεργάζεται η **[ΕΠΩΝΥΜΙΑ ΕΠΙΧΕΙΡΗΣΗΣ]**, ή για την άσκηση άλλων δικαιωμάτων σας, επικοινωνήστε με το **[EMAIL ΑΠΟΡΡΗΤΟΥ ΕΠΙΧΕΙΡΗΣΗΣ]** ή απαντήστε στην υπάρχουσα συνομιλία εξυπηρέτησης. Περιγράψτε συνοπτικά το αίτημά σας. Για λόγους ασφαλείας, ενδέχεται να ζητήσουμε μόνο τα αναγκαία στοιχεία επιβεβαίωσης ταυτότητας. Θα απαντήσουμε εντός των νόμιμων προθεσμιών. Ορισμένα δεδομένα ενδέχεται να πρέπει να διατηρηθούν όταν αυτό επιτρέπεται ή απαιτείται από τον νόμο.

Αριθμός αναφοράς: **[OPAQUE CASE REF]**. Μη στέλνετε έγγραφα ταυτότητας χωρίς συγκεκριμένο ασφαλές αίτημά μας.

## 3. Staff quick process (all companies)

1. Customer contacts **their business**, not Sendro.
2. Staff opens that business's GDPR Manager tenant, creates a new case with *only* the source channel, copies the random case reference into the existing restricted support email/thread. **Never put name, phone, email, message, reservation ID or documents in the GDPR case metadata.**
3. Record time of receipt; one-calendar-month guidance is not a final legal deadline calculation for every scenario.
4. Independently assess identity/authority if needed; only mark confirmation after a real check. Review affected systems, legal holds/retention obligations, and provider copies. The UI does **not** discover or erase customer records.
5. The business sends the actual response outside Sendro; **only then** records that response was sent in the manager.
6. "STOP" / unsubscribe marketing is a distinct opt-out request; follow the business messaging consent process. Do not treat it as an automatic request to erase accounting/reservation records.

Before any company launch: verify privacy notice, actual contact email, DPA with Sendro, data inventory, security, retention, and real escalation/fulfillment procedures. Legal review recommended; this is not a compliance certificate.
